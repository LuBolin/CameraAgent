# Keep camera authority and pixels on-device

The remote model is an enum-only language classifier: no preview or captured pixels leave the phone, and diagnosis, user-facing wording, capability checks, planning, camera execution, and verification remain in the Android app. This sacrifices multimodal flexibility and model-written replies, but makes the privacy promise, offline path, testable safety boundary, and camera-control authority explicit; reversing it would require redesigning consent, the threat model, the gateway contract, evaluation, and the product's trust model.
