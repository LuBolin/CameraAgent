# Photo Helper — Exact Backup Demo Video Script

Target runtime: **2:40–2:50**  
Stage command: **“Make the picture brighter, focus on the panda, and take a photo in five seconds.”**

## 0:00–0:10 — Open on the product

**Picture:** Real Android phone aimed at the panda. `PHOTO HELPER` appears briefly over live footage.

**Voice-over:**

> “People know the photo they want, but not always the camera setting that creates it.”

## 0:10–0:22 — Product promise

**Picture:** Clean ready state with the panda, Mic, shutter and sparkle visible.

**Voice-over:**

> “Photo Helper turns a spoken outcome into a safe, reversible camera plan on Android.”

## 0:22–0:37 — Spoken goal

**Picture:** Tap Mic. Show listening and the square Stop control. Subtitle the request exactly.

**Live command audio:**

> “Make the picture brighter, focus on the panda, and take a photo in five seconds.”

Do not place narration over the command.

## 0:37–0:55 — Agent planning

**Picture:** Transcript followed by Qwen planning. If dead time is shortened, show `NETWORK WAIT SHORTENED`.

**Voice-over:**

> “Qwen combines the request with a clean planning frame and current camera telemetry. It returns an ordered semantic plan—not direct camera commands.”

## 0:55–1:25 — Phone execution

**Picture:** Visible exposure adjustment, yellow reticle on the panda, countdown and capture.

**Voice-over until the countdown reaches three:**

> “Android checks every step against the action contract and the active camera. It applies the supported change, grounds focus on the panda and starts the capture.”

Let the last three countdown beats and shutter sound play cleanly.

## 1:25–1:40 — Human control

**Picture:** Capture result, then Reset restoring the baseline. Include a brief close-up of Stop if it was not visible earlier.

**Voice-over:**

> “The user remains in control. Stop interrupts an active run, and Reset restores the camera baseline.”

## 1:40–1:58 — Smart Mode

**Picture:** Tap the sparkle button. Show `Make this shot look nicer` and the resulting conservative adjustment or `Looks good already` state.

**Voice-over:**

> “Smart Mode helps when the user knows something looks wrong but does not know what to ask. One tap lets Qwen safely adjust exposure, colour, framing or focus.”

## 1:58–2:18 — Authority boundary

**Picture:** One flow only: `SPEAK → PLAN → CHECK → ACT → RESET`, followed by `AI proposes · Phone decides · Human controls`.

**Voice-over:**

> “The boundary is deliberate. AI proposes a bounded plan. Android validates and executes it through CameraX. The human starts, stops and resets.”

## 2:18–2:33 — WorkBuddy

**Picture:** Clean WorkBuddy workflow: `HY3 IMPLEMENTATION → SPECIALIST REVIEW → PRODUCT VERIFICATION`.

**Voice-over:**

> “WorkBuddy accelerated implementation and strengthened product quality, with specialist review across architecture, UX and QA.”

## 2:33–2:45 — Value

**Picture:** Completed panda photo, then simple labels: `FAMILIES · CASUAL PHOTOGRAPHERS · ACCESSIBILITY` and `CAMERA MODE · OEM SDK`.

**Voice-over:**

> “Photo Helper starts with everyday photographers and can grow into an assistive camera mode or SDK for device makers and camera apps.”

## 2:45–2:50 — Close

**Picture:** Final one-page poster.

**Voice-over:**

> “Photo Helper: speak in outcomes, not camera settings.”
