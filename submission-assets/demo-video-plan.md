# Photo Helper — Backup Demo Video Structure

Target runtime: **2:40–2:50**  
Maximum runtime: **2:55**  
Format: **16:9 H.264/AAC, 1080p or native landscape resolution, 30 fps**

This is a product demo, not a shortened presentation. The real camera flow should occupy most of the runtime. Use the panda, the same stage phone and the same compound request as the live demo.

## Content priorities

1. Panda flow on a real Android phone.
2. Visible grounding: the yellow reticle must clearly sit on the panda.
3. Countdown and physical capture at normal speed.
4. Stop and Reset as visible human controls.
5. Smart Mode as a short supporting feature.
6. One simple architecture boundary.
7. WorkBuddy as implementation plus specialist review.
8. Business route and close.

## What to remove from the current backup

- Long cover holds.
- Multiple problem slides saying the same thing.
- Selfie or unrelated example clips.
- Code, terminal output and test-count screens.
- Internal approval, changelog or agent-management language.
- More than one architecture diagram.
- Any sequence created by trimming the first portion of the old four-minute presentation.

## Editing rules

- Show the first real product action within five seconds.
- Keep one device, one panda, one orientation and one request through the hero flow.
- Preserve the real spoken request, countdown and shutter sound.
- Shorten network waiting only; label it `NETWORK WAIT SHORTENED`.
- Do not splice separate requests to look like one compound plan.
- Use captions throughout and manually correct `Qwen`, `CameraX`, `WorkBuddy` and `HY3`.
- Keep on-screen copy to one short claim at a time.
- Use simple cuts and restrained crossfades. No decorative transitions.
- End on the final poster for at least four seconds.
